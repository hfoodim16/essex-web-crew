About us

A certified arborist your neighbors already trust.

Happy Trees by MGM has been the local, owner-operated choice for tree work in Bloomfield, Montclair and Nutley since 2003.

Marvin's story

Founded in 2003 by Marvin Monge.

Happy Trees strives to be the leading tree service provider in Bloomfield, Montclair and Nutley, New Jersey. The company was founded by Marvin Monge, a 20-year veteran of the tree service industry.

Marvin is an ISA-accredited arborist — meaning the person deciding how your tree gets cut is trained in the science of keeping trees healthy, not just taking them down. That certification, paired with more than two decades on the ropes, is why so much of our work comes from neighbors recommending neighbors.

Fully licensed and insured, with workers' compensation included on every job, Happy Trees is built to do careful work safely — and to clean up like we were never there.
