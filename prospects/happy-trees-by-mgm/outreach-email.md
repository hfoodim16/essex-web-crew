## Pitch One-Pager

### What's working against you online right now

- Dated, non-responsive WordPress site. Theme assets last updated 2017, duplicate navigation menus in the code, and the layout degrades badly on mobile.
- ISA arborist credential and NJ license (NJTC 768421) are buried. These are your strongest differentiators and most tree services don't have them. They're not visible on the first scroll.
- Permits and free wood chip delivery are hidden in the nav, not featured anywhere prominent. Both are real differentiators most competitors don't offer.
- Reviews live off-site. Strong ratings on Nextdoor, Angi (4.9 stars), and HomeAdvisor (5.0 stars) but none showcased on your owned page.
- No LocalBusiness schema, no service + town meta titles for searches like "tree removal Bloomfield" or "arborist Montclair NJ."

### What the mockup shows

- 4-page static site: Home / Services / About / Contact & Get a Quote
- Hero: *ISA-Accredited Arborist · Bloomfield, Montclair, Nutley · Founded 2003*
- Trust strip: 23 years, ISA-accredited, NJTC 768421, licensed & insured, free estimates, visible on load
- Services: Tree Removal & Stumps / Tree Care & Pruning / Emergency Storm Response / Extras (holiday lighting, free wood chip delivery, permit handling for municipal removals)
- About section: Marvin's story, 20-year veteran, ISA credential, licensed and insured, workers' comp included
- Reviews: real Nextdoor quotes (R.U. Bloomfield, L.T. Cedar Grove) on your owned page
- Tap-to-call (main and emergency) and quote form throughout; "5% off over $500 first-timer" promo in Contact

### The maintenance story

Static site. No WordPress to patch, no plugin updates, no monthly CMS fees. It runs itself.
