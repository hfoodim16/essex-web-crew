1961

The year it started.

Cedar Grove Transmissions & Auto Repair Inc. in Cedar Grove, NJ provides quality auto repair and transmission services. We are a family owned and operated company that has been in business for over 60 years.

That kind of longevity in one town isn't an accident. It's what happens when drivers keep coming back — and send their kids and neighbors — because they got a straight answer and fair work, year after year. The operator our customers know by name is Mike, and the reviews below tell that story better than we can.

We lead with transmissions because that's our specialty and our name, but we're a full-service shop: general repair for foreign and domestic vehicles, maintenance, and the complimentary road test that makes sure we're fixing the right thing.
