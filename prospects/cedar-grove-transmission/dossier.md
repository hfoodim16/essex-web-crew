## Art-direction hints

"Honest, grounded, blue-collar-premium." A confident automotive palette — deep graphite /
near-black + a single strong accent (signal red or a trustworthy steel blue) + a clean warm
neutral. Clean, mechanical-but-refined type: an industrial-leaning display (not Inter/
Roboto) + a highly legible humanist sans. Personality words: **honest, seasoned,
dependable.** Lean into "since 1961" heritage without looking dated.
