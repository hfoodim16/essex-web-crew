The Firm.

Anthony's Landscaping was founded by Anthony Molinaro over thirty years ago and has served South Orange, Maplewood and the surrounding Essex County ever since. In 2025, ownership passed to Chike Achebe, who continues the company's tradition — retaining the experienced crew and the same commitment to quality, experience, workmanship, and professional integrity. We provide outstanding professional service and complete satisfaction, from start to finish: design, construction, hardscape and full-property care, all from one firm that is there every step of the way.
