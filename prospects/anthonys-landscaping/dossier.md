## Business summary

Full-service design-build landscaping company in South Orange, NJ, founded **over 30
years ago** by Anthony Molinaro, with a strong reputation across the Maplewood/South
Orange (SOMA) area. It has the **widest service breadth in the shortlist** — design,
construction, hardscape, driveways, drainage, and full maintenance — which makes it the
richest multi-page showcase. Critically, it **has a live website
(anthonyslandscapingnj.com) we can capture real content from** — but the site is an
early-2000s, table-based build with `.gif` navigation and no mobile framework. The
before→after here is "respected 30-year firm, ancient website."

## Current-presence critique

- **Early-2000s build:** table-based layout, `.gif` image navigation, no responsive
  framework — **breaks on mobile**, where most local searches now happen.
- Ancient design actively undercuts a premium 30-year design-build firm — the site looks
  cheaper than the work.
- Long flat service list with no visual hierarchy; no project gallery to prove craftsmanship.
- `contact.php` form only; no map, no hours, no reviews surfaced.
- Ownership/branding ambiguity (see discrepancy above) signals a neglected web presence.

## Art-direction hints

"Refined design-build, established craftsmanship." A confident, slightly upscale take:
deep green + warm stone/bluestone gray + charcoal + a brass/gold accent for a premium
feel. Strong display serif or high-contrast sans for headings; clean sans body. Let big
hardscape/landscape photography carry it. The goal: make a 30-year firm finally look as
premium as its patios.
