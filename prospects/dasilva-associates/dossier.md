page through Google's machine translation client-side. The only human-written
multilingual text anywhere on the site is the header line **"Se Habla Espanol / Nos
falamos o portugues."**

**Implication for the page map: build 4 English pages (Home, Practice Areas, Attorney
Bio, Contact), not real PT/ES page sets** — there is no genuine bilingual content to

translate-widget is not something to replicate or improve — it's parity-neutral to drop
(see site-content.md).
