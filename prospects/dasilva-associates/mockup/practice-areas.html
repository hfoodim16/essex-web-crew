The aim is to reduce or dismiss the charge and keep points off the license. On a DWI he also handles the administrative process of protecting your driving privileges.

Common violations, and what they carry

Driving without a license
A fine of $200 to $500 or imprisonment for not more than 60 days, and no license issued for at least 180 days.
Driving on a suspended license
A fine of $500, a surcharge of $250 for 3 years, and a 6-month license suspension.
Reckless driving
A fine of $50 to $200 or imprisonment not exceeding 60 days, or both, even on a first offense.
Driving uninsured
A fine of $300 to $1,000, a period of community service as determined by the court, and suspension of driving privileges for 1 year on a first offense.
Speeding
A tiered penalty system.

These are the penalties the statutes set. What a court imposes in a given case is a separate question, and it's the one worth calling about.
