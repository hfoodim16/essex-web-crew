# Site content capture: Atlas-Law (www.matlas-law.com)

Captured 2026-10-12 by the analyst (capture-only mode). Fetched with plain HTTP GET (curl) on public pages only. No forms submitted, no one contacted.

## What the domain shows

- `http://matlas-law.com`, `https://matlas-law.com`, `http://www.matlas-law.com` all redirect (200) to `https://www.matlas-law.com/`.
- **Platform:** Squarespace (`<!-- This is Squarespace. -->` in source). DNS nameservers `nsb1-4.squarespacedns.com`. MX records are Google Workspace (`aspmx.l.google.com`), which fits `matt@matlas-law.com` being a live mailbox.
- **What it is:** a Squarespace **AI-generated starter draft**. The copy is generic template text, every image is a Squarespace-generated file (`imgg-gg3p-…png`), the favicon is the Squarespace default, the footer social icons link to **Squarespace's own** Instagram/X/Facebook, and the "Services" page is a **Squarespace Commerce store** with four placeholder products and prices. There is no attorney name, no phone, no email, no address and no bar admission anywhere on it. Footer: "ATLAS LAW / Made with Squarespace".
- **Navigation:** Services (`/services-store`), About (`/about`), Contact (`/contact`), plus a cart (`/cart`, empty).

### Content-parity verdict

**Nothing on this site is client-authored content that needs carrying.** It is template filler the client didn't write (Q12–Q14 of the questionnaire are blank: nothing he likes, nothing that bugs him, nothing to keep word-for-word). **Content parity for this build is the client's answers in `client-answers.md`.** The verbatim text is preserved below anyway so the Planner can list it on the "Deliberately dropped" list with a reason, and so nobody mistakes a template claim for a fact. Two template claims in particular must **not** reach the new site as facts:

- "For more than 20 years, Atlas has delivered strategic legal and business counsel…" is template copy. The client's own answer is "over 15 years as an in-house counsel" (see dossier, Confirm with client).
- The four priced "products" ($95 / $750 / $399 / $350) are Squarespace sample products. The client's own fee position is Q11: "open to Fixed Fee/Subscription arrangements". No prices were given.
