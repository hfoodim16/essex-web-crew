About your business

2. How long have you been operating, and what's the short story? (A sentence or two: who started it, how it grew.)
I am just launching my own firm as of September. I have been back in private practice since last September after spending over 15 years as an in-house counsel. Really looking to create some momentum and attract new clients. And most importantly letting people know that this is what I’m doing now and am available if they are looking for legal services.

3. What makes you different from the other guys? (The thing you'd want every customer to know, said the way you'd say it to a customer.)
I have spent over 15 years as an in-house counsel at major corporations (JPMorgan Chase, Colgate-Palmolive, Saks Fifth Avenue, and Related). That experience allows me to understand the client-side well. This is a different perspective and lens than a lot of outside counsel who have spent the majority of their careers in private practice. As a legal and business advisor, I’ve helped clients solve their problems. Provide practical solutions and avoid legal being the obstacle to getting things done.

6. Who are your best customers? (Homeowners, businesses, a certain town or type of job, whoever you'd like more of.)
The clients that I am really trying to attract are small to medium sized businesses ($50-200MM in annual revenue) that do not have their own in-house legal departments. These are companies that have legal issues but don’t have the need or the funds for a full-time in house lawyer. As a result they are very reliant upon outside counsel that will inevitably be far more expensive and possibly less skilled/experienced as me.
