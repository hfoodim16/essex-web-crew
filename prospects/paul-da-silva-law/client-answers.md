### Art direction — the one instruction this file does give the planner

Break the legal-industry color convention **deliberately and say so in `website-plan.md`**. The
category default is navy + gold + serif, which reads as every other firm site and cannot clear the
critic's boldness ≥ 8. Per `skills/web-design-ultra/references/color-conventions.md`, make the
honor-or-break call explicitly and justify it: we are building an **Ironbound identity** — a Newark
Portuguese-district firm — while keeping enough gravity that a defendant's family reads it as
serious. Do not lose the gravity chasing the flourish.

**Anti-repetition (last 3 signed rows of `design-memory.md` are banned):**

| Banned | Fonts | Palette | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cedar-grove-transmission` | Archivo / Barlow | industrial graphite + steel + red | spec-sheet + bento |
| `happy-trees-by-mgm` | Zilla Slab / Work Sans | bark + moss + sky + hi-vis lime | canopy-descent full-bleed |
| `fora-digital` | Instrument Serif / Hanken Grotesk | warm-editorial paper + ink + **cobalt** + clay | gallery-wall plates |

⚠ **Collision risk to design around:** the obvious azulejo tile-blue lands close to fora-digital's
cobalt, and a warm limestone ground lands close to its paper-and-ink. Push the accent somewhere it
can't be confused — port/oxblood, or the deep green of the Portuguese flag — rather than resolving
into another blue-on-paper site.

`fade-up` and number `count-up` are flagged defaults. Pick a real signature move from
`skills/web-design-ultra/references/motion.md`. All motion `prefers-reduced-motion`-gated.
