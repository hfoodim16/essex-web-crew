Attorney

Paul Da Silva

Founder, DaSilva & Associates, LLC, in Newark's Ironbound.

▣
Photo of Paul Da Silva — real headshot to come.

Paul Da Silva was born and raised in New Jersey, the son of immigrant parents. He is fluent in both Portuguese and Spanish. At a firm in the heart of Newark's Ironbound, that means a client's family can walk in and be understood in their own language.

He founded DaSilva & Associates in May 2002, after litigating criminal cases, including murder trials, at the New Jersey Office of the Public Defender, and after work at two Legal Aid offices and a boutique litigation firm in Montclair. His practice covers criminal defense, traffic, family law, and real estate.
