The office

Since 2002, one case at a time.

DaSilva & Associates opened in May 2002. Every case here is handled personally. A real estate closing or a traffic ticket may be routine for the office, but it rarely feels that way from where you're sitting.

The approach is practical and precise: advocate firmly where it counts, and avoid running up needless litigation costs where it doesn't.
