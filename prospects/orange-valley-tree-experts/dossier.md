## Business summary

A **family-owned tree service in Verona, Essex County**, founded — by their own words on their own
website — **in 1976**, which makes 2026 their **50th year**. Three services, deep: **tree removal,
tree pruning, stump grinding**, backed by cranes, bucket trucks and climbers, with **24-hour
emergency service** and free consultations.

The owner is **Kevin Papocchia**, and his wife **Karen** runs the office. That is not an inference —
they say it themselves on their homepage. Kevin is a **Licensed Tree Care Operator (LTCO #456)**,
and the business holds **NJ Board of Tree Experts registration #NJTC791091**.

What separates them from the average two-truck outfit is the **client roster**, which they publish
in one sentence and then never elaborate on: they prune and remove trees for *"several local school
districts, nursery schools, churches, synagogues, condominium complexes, health care facilities,
nursing homes, museums, and many more."* That is nine categories of repeat institutional work — the
kind of book of business a homeowner cannot see anywhere, because the site never names a single one.

**Currently active, and verifiable.** They appear on **Verona Township's official 2026 licensed tree
contractor list** (I fetched it myself; the page is stamped *"Updated 7.30.26"* and they are #7 of
20). Their domain was **renewed on 2026-04-19** and runs through 2027. They are paying for the
website right now.
