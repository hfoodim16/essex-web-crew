**"I'm busy right now"**

"Completely understand. That's exactly why I'm asking to just send the link, not pitch you on the phone. What's a good cell or email? You can look on your own time."

"Happy to talk numbers after you see the mockup. But the short version: it's a static site, so once it's up there's basically nothing to maintain. No monthly platform fee, no subscription. Building it is one conversation."

"That makes sense. Sounds like most of your clients come through word of mouth, which is a good sign. The only thing I'd say is that anyone who Googles 'CPA Bloomfield' right now doesn't land on your page. But I appreciate your time -- if it ever comes up, I'm easy to reach."

"Great -- what's the best number or email?" *(Get it. Read it back to confirm.)* "I'll get it over to you within the hour. If you have questions after you look, I'm at [Harry's phone]."

**What's weak about his current presence:**
- No owned website. He's been in business since 1983, and Googling his name returns a localsearch.com directory proxy.
- No page explaining what he does, who he helps, or how to reach him during tax season.
- Warm Nextdoor reputation ("The Best!") scattered across directories, invisible on any page he controls.
- Contact is a phone number on aggregators. No form, no hours, no map.

**What the mockup shows:**
- 3-page static site: Home / Services / About & Contact
- Hero: *John Sessa, CPA · Bloomfield, NJ · Serving clients since 1983*
- Trust strip: 43 years, CPA credential, by the Bloomfield train station
- Services block: Tax Prep & Planning · Bookkeeping · Accounting
- One real Nextdoor testimonial (T.B., Nutley: "straight and to the point, and very good at what he does")
- Contact section: phone, address, appointment placeholder

**The maintenance story:**
Static site. No CMS to update, no subscription, no platform fee. He builds it once and doesn't touch it again.
