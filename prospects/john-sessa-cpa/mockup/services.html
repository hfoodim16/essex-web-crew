Services

Services — plain and simple.

Everything a solo CPA does best: careful tax work, clean books, and a real person to ask. Here's how John helps individuals and small businesses across Essex County.

01 — Tax

Tax Preparation & Planning

The heart of the practice. Individual and small-business returns prepared carefully, checked, and filed on time — with year-round planning so nothing about April catches you off guard.

  • Individual (1040) tax preparation
  • Small-business & self-employed returns
  • Multi-state and complex-return situations
  • Year-round tax planning & questions
02 — Books

Accounting & Bookkeeping

General accounting and bookkeeping for small businesses — organized, accurate records you can actually use to make decisions, and that make tax time painless.

  • Small-business bookkeeping
  • General ledger & account reconciliation
  • Financial record organization
  • Books kept clean and tax-ready year-round
Tidy small-business bookkeeping scene — organized receipts, ledger, laptop, warm light
03 — Advisory

Consulting & Business Planning

Sometimes the question isn't strictly a tax question. When it comes up, John can talk it through with you — from small-business decisions to planning ahead.

Not sure if this fits? Ask John whether advisory support makes sense for your situation — every practice is a little different, and he'll tell you straight.
