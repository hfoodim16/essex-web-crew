About

A Bloomfield CPA since 1983.

John Sessa Jr is a Certified Public Accountant who has run his own practice in Bloomfield, New Jersey since 1983 — more than four decades of tax and accounting work for the people and small businesses of Essex County.

It's a solo practice, and that's the point. You work with John himself — the person who prepares your return, keeps your books, and answers the phone when a question comes up. No account-manager handoffs, no rotating staff. Just a CPA who knows your name and your numbers.

The office sits at 142 Washington Street, right by the Bloomfield train station — easy to reach whether you're around the corner or riding in from a neighboring town. Much of the practice is longtime clients who keep coming back, which is about the best thing a CPA can say for himself.

The work is straightforward: get your taxes right, keep your books clean, and give you a straight answer when you need one.
