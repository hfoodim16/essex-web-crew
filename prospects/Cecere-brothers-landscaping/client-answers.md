## About your business

**1. What's the business name, exactly as it should appear on the site?**

Cecere Brothers Landscaping, LLC & Green Lawns Sprinkler, LLC

**2. How long have you been operating, and what's the short story?**

Cecere Brothers Landscaping, LLC is owned and operated by **Michael Cecere**, whose passion
for landscaping began in 1976 at the age of 14. What started with mowing lawns in his
neighborhood and completing small landscape projects grew into a lifelong career dedicated
to creating and maintaining beautiful outdoor spaces.

Michael attended the **University of Delaware**, where he studied engineering, before
transferring to **Montclair State University**. He graduated in 1985 with a **degree in
Business Administration with a concentration in Finance**. Following graduation, Michael
turned his lifelong passion into a business and founded Cecere Brothers Landscaping.

In 1989, Michael and his childhood friend and neighbor, **Thomas Verrone**, founded **Green
Lawns Sprinkler, LLC**. Green Lawns specializes in underground irrigation systems designed
to provide lawns, plants, and landscapes with the water they need while promoting efficient
water usage.

Today, Cecere Brothers Landscaping and Green Lawns Sprinkler have grown into a team of **15
experienced professionals** providing comprehensive landscaping and irrigation services.
From landscape design and installation to lawn maintenance, fertilization, pruning, masonry,
and irrigation, our team handles every project **in-house, without subcontractors**.

### Local Experienced Professionals You Can Trust

Our long-standing team is one of the foundations of our success. Many of our employees have
been with us for 20 years or more, bringing valuable experience, craftsmanship, and a
commitment to doing the job right.

- **Joe Collura** joined the company in 1993 and manages our lawn fertilization programs,
  shrub pruning crew, and small-jobs crew.
- **Cesar Sanchez** joined us in 2005 and handles property maintenance, landscape design,
  plantings, mulching, and a variety of other landscape services.
- **Jose Pulido** joined us in 2010 and leads our masonry crews. His work includes steps,
  walkways, patios, walls, fireplaces, fire pits, outdoor kitchens, and more.
- **Marcos Castro** has been with Green Lawns Sprinkler since 2015 and leads our sprinkler
  installation and service crew. His son, **Jeremy Castro**, joined the company in 2022.

The longevity and dedication of our employees reflect the values that have guided our
company for decades: **quality workmanship, personal service, reliability, and pride in what
we do.**

### A Tradition Built to Last

From a neighborhood lawn-mowing business started by a 14-year-old with a passion for
landscaping to a full-service landscaping and irrigation company, Cecere Brothers Landscaping
and Green Lawns Sprinkler have remained committed to one simple goal: **providing our
customers with exceptional service and beautiful, lasting results.**

With decades of experience, a dedicated in-house team, and a personal commitment to every
project, we take pride in creating outdoor spaces that our customers can enjoy for years to
come.
