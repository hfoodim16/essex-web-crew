## Watch list (on top of banlist Tier 1 and Tier 1B)
- `grounds` — estate word; a Caldwell homeowner has a yard, not grounds. Was used 3× in
  visible copy (hero, gallery heading, closing CTA). **Target: zero.**
- `tend` / `tending` — same drift ("What we tend to").
- `design-led` — agency vocabulary.

## Cutesy is the failure mode this site keeps reverting to

Two rounds of copy on this site drifted whimsical, not purple — and whimsy passes every
mechanical check because it's plain and conversational. Watch specifically for:

- **Plants and weather with feelings** — "won't need babying", "the lawn woken up".
- **The crew given mystic perception** — "we read the sun".
- **Landscaping puns** — "Rooted in West Essex", "local roots".
- **Winks** — "Three steps, no mystery", "a lawn that looks handled, because it is".
- **Jokes about the work** — "past the point of no return", "a mess and a deadline".

The owner's standard, in his words: *it's a landscaper, not a dessert.* If a line makes
the reader notice the writing, it's wrong for this site.
