Cecere Brothers Landscaping and Green Lawns Sprinkler run as one team. Landscaping or irrigation, you're dealing with the same crew.

A tradition built to last.

Michael Cecere began mowing lawns in his West Caldwell neighborhood in 1976 at the age of 14. Michael graduated James Caldwell High School in 1980 and went on to study engineering at the University of Delaware.

He transferred to Montclair State University, and graduated in 1985 with a degree in Business Administration and a concentration in Finance. Following graduation, he turned his passion for lawn care and landscaping into Cecere Brothers Landscaping.

In 1989, Michael and his childhood friend and neighbor Thomas Verrone founded Green Lawns Sprinkler, LLC. Green Lawns installs and services underground irrigation systems that promote efficient water use and give lawns, plants, and landscapes the water they need to flourish.

Today the two companies run as one team of 15 dedicated employees with over 150 years of combined experience. Every project is handled in-house, without subcontractors. A local company with local ownership, built through word of mouth and referrals, has grown through our thousands of jobs successfully completed in the West Essex area.

Workmanship, personal service, reliability, and pride in what we do.
