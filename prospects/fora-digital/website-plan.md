## 1. Art direction — **"Main Street Modern" (warm gallery)**

A small-town gallery hung with modern work. Warm linen paper, oversized elegant serif
headlines, hairline frame linework — and the portfolio pieces presented like framed
plates on a gallery wall, each in a browser-chrome frame. The site itself is the proof:
it must *be* a modern site a landscaper would trust — warm, legible, confident, zero
tech-bro intimidation. The dark, moody portfolio screenshots (both are near-black heroes)
pop hard against the light warm wall; a dark site would swallow them.

Personality words: **honest · warm · crafted · confident · local.**

### Occupational color-convention call — **BREAK, deliberately**

- **The convention:** web agencies default to dark-first / tech-slick / gradient — near-black
  backgrounds, neon or purple gradients, huge grotesque type, glow orbs. Psychology:
  cutting-edge competence, "we live in the future."
- **The call: BREAK it.** Two reasons, both structural:
  1. **Our buyers are local trade businesses** (landscapers, CPAs, transmission shops, tree
     services — see CLAUDE.md territory). A 55-year-old owner-operator comparing us on his
     phone should feel "these people get my business," not "this is a Brooklyn crypto shop."
     A site that alienates a landscaper is wrong even if it wins design points.
  2. **Both portfolio screenshots are dark.** On a dark site they vanish; on warm linen they
     read as dramatic framed artwork. The palette serves the portfolio, which is the site's
     stated #1 job.
- **Trust cue kept from the category** (required when breaking): a single **electric cobalt
  blue** as the working accent — the tech world's trust color — carried in CTAs, links,
  badges, and the wordmark mark. So it still reads "capable modern web people," just warm
  ones.

### Stage 3 evidence sheet (patterns extracted, none copied)

| Source | Pattern taken |
|---|---|

| sanctuary.computer | Plain-spoken confidence ("we don't broker the work, we do the work"); footer transparency about how the studio works; visual-first project cards with one-line descriptors. Counter-lesson: they anonymize the team — we do the OPPOSITE (two named founders IS our story) |
| siegemedia.com | Benefit-first hero headline; clear section order (hero → work → CTA); trust via specifics. Counter-lesson: their trust = big metrics/logos — we have one client, so our trust move is radical honesty (labeled badges) instead of scale claims |
| Our own Cecere + Steakhouse mockups (viewed) | Both heroes are dark with serif display type → light warm frame maximizes contrast; browser-chrome framing tells "this is a real website" at a glance |

### Stage 4 — anti-repetition: banned combos avoided (crew `design-memory.md`, last 3)

- **john-sessa-cpa** — Spectral / Public Sans · precision porcelain + ink-navy + manila +
  verdigris · sidebar-anchored letterhead · dot-grid graph paper. **Avoided:** different
  fonts; our palette is warm linen + *electric cobalt* + clay (warm temperature, saturated
  pop) vs their cool hushed navy/verdigris family; layout is a gallery-wall one-pager, not
  sidebar letterhead; background is paper grain + frame rules, not dot-grid.
- **cedar-grove-transmission** — Archivo / Barlow · industrial graphite + steel + red ·
  spec-sheet + bento · schematic linework. **Avoided:** no Archivo/Barlow, light not
  graphite, no bento, no schematic linework.
- **happy-trees-by-mgm** — Zilla Slab / Work Sans · bark + moss + sky + hi-vis lime ·
  canopy-descent full-bleed · dapple light. **Avoided:** no slab, no green-nature family,
  no full-bleed photo scroll, no dapple.
- Also steered clear of older crew rows: no Fraunces (used twice), no Marcellus/Mulish,
  no Bricolage/Nunito.
- **Motion:** `fade-up` and `count-up` are flagged defaults — **not used** (signature is
  mask-curtain; there are no stats to count anyway).

### Stage 5 — the three divergent directions (differ on ≥3 of 5 axes)

**Direction 1 — "Main Street Modern" (warm gallery) — CHOSEN**
- Concept: a warm gallery wall for local-business websites; the anti-agency agency site.
- Layout: editorial-asymmetric one-pager, portfolio as offset framed plates.
- Type: **Instrument Serif** display / **Hanken Grotesk** body.
- Palette: warm linen + ink + electric cobalt + clay (light-warm, one sharp accent). **BREAKS**
  the dark-agency convention; keeps cobalt as the category trust cue.
- Background: linen paper grain + hairline frame linework + one soft warm radial wash.
- Motion: smooth-editorial — **mask-curtain** entrances, **ink-sweep** hovers, one
  scroll-scrub "gallery hang" set-piece.
- Why bold: breaking the entire category's dark-slick uniform IS the bold move; poster-scale
  serif at 7rem; honest one-client portfolio worn proudly as framed evidence.

**Direction 2 — "Night Shift" (honors convention) — REJECTED**
- Concept: dark-first slick studio; charcoal, gradient glow orbs, grotesque + mono type
  (Space Grotesk / JetBrains Mono), kinetic marquees, immersive full-bleed scroll.
- **HONORS** the dark/tech-slick agency convention (the required honor-option of the three).
- Rejected because: (a) it intimidates exactly the local trade owners we sell to; (b) the
  two dark portfolio screenshots sink into a dark page — the site's #1 job fails; (c)
  honoring this convention makes us look like every other agency, which for a two-person
  local studio is camouflage, not credibility.

**Direction 3 — "Double Byline" (split-screen duo) — REJECTED**
- Concept: the two-founder story as a literal split-screen site — two color fields (one per
  founder), high-contrast duotone, geometric sans (Familjen Grotesk), playful-springy
  slide-alternate motion, flat whitespace background. **BREAKS** convention (playful duotone).
- Rejected because: the gimmick centers the founders' *gimmick* over the portfolio (the
  stated #1 job); a hard duotone fights both dark screenshots; and with only 4 work cards
  and 2 bios, a split-screen system runs out of content to justify itself.

Axes check: each pair of directions differs on layout, typography, color strategy,
background, and motion — ≥4 of 5 axes apart. None touches a banned combo.
**Pick: Direction 1** — the boldest that serves the brief's actual audience and assets.

## 3. Typography

- **Display: Instrument Serif** (Google Fonts; weights: 400 + 400 italic — it is a
  single-weight face; use *italic* for emphasis words, never faux-bold).
  Used for: H1/H2/H3, plate titles, monogram initials, the wordmark "Fora Digital",
  pull-lines. Tight leading (1.02–1.08 on display sizes), letter-spacing -0.01em.
- **Body: Hanken Grotesk** (weights 400, 500, 600, 700).
  Used for: body copy, nav, buttons, kickers/eyebrows (600, uppercase, +0.14em tracking),
  captions, badges, footer.
- Load via Google Fonts `<link>` with `display=swap`, preconnect both font hosts.
- **Never** Inter/Roboto/Arial/Helvetica anywhere, including fallback-first stacks.
  Fallback stacks: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif` and
  `'Segoe UI', system-ui, sans-serif`.

### Type scale (CSS custom properties)

```css
--fs-hero:    clamp(3.4rem, 9vw, 7.25rem);   /* H1 — poster scale */
--fs-h2:      clamp(2.1rem, 4.5vw, 3.5rem);
--fs-h3:      clamp(1.35rem, 2vw, 1.65rem);
--fs-lead:    clamp(1.125rem, 1.5vw, 1.3125rem);
--fs-body:    1.0625rem;
--fs-small:   0.9375rem;
--fs-kicker:  0.8125rem;   /* uppercase, +0.14em, Hanken 600 */

```

## 4. Color system (full palette as `:root` tokens)

```css
:root {
  /* Main Street Modern — warm gallery. BREAKS the dark-agency convention;
     cobalt kept as the category trust cue. */
  --paper:   #F5F1E8;  /* page background — warm linen */
  --surface: #FDFBF5;  /* cards / plates / header bar — lifted cream */
  --ink:     #1B1813;  /* primary text — warm near-black */
  --stone:   #6E675C;  /* secondary text, captions (AA on --paper: 5.4:1) */
  --cobalt:  #2447C2;  /* accent — CTAs, links, badges, marks, wordmark dot */
  --cobalt-deep: #1B36A0; /* hover/active state of cobalt */
  --clay:    #B4552D;  /* rare warm highlight — hero emphasis word, small rules */
  --line:    #DDD5C4;  /* hairline frames, borders, dividers */
}
```

- **Roles:** `--paper` background · `--ink` text · `--cobalt` the ONLY interactive color ·
  `--clay` decorative highlight only (never on interactive elements) · `--line` for the
  gallery frame linework.
- **Contrast pairs (builder must hold these):** ink on paper ≈ 14:1 ✓ · white `#FFFFFF`
  text on cobalt ≈ 6.9:1 ✓ (buttons/badges) · cobalt text on paper ≈ 6.4:1 ✓ (links) ·
  stone on paper only at `--fs-small` and up ✓ · clay is used at display sizes (≥2rem) or
  as non-text rules only — never small body text, never on buttons.
- Restraint rule: cobalt appears exactly where a click lives (+ the two badges/wordmark
  mark); clay appears ≤3 times on the whole page.

## 8. Background & atmosphere direction

Light pages die flat — this one layers three free treatments (from the skill's
`backgrounds.md`; keep all subtle, this is a gallery not a rave):

1. **Linen grain** — full-page SVG feTurbulence noise overlay at ~3% opacity multiply
   (one inline SVG data-URI, `position: fixed`, `pointer-events: none`).
2. **Hairline frame linework** — the hero's inset mat rectangle + section divider rules +
   the plate frames, all `--line`. The linework IS the layout's signature garnish.
3. **One warm radial wash** — a single large radial gradient (`#FBE8D0` at 45% opacity →
   transparent) behind the hero headline, off-center top-left; a second, cooler faint wash
   (`#DCE4FA` 30%) behind the Contact section. Static, no animation.

No fog/god-rays/motes — atmosphere effects read "moody dark site"; this direction's air
is paper and daylight. (Deliberate omission, not an oversight.)

## 10. Signature motion (named recipes from `references/motion.md`)

- **Entrance family (ONE): `mask-curtain`** — a `--cobalt` panel slides off each revealed
  element (`transform-origin: right`, `.7s var(--ease-curtain)`), used on: hero headline
  lines (staggered ~90ms per line via `--i`), section H2s, plate frames, founder cards.
  On the hero it reads as work being *unveiled* — the gallery concept made literal.
  Explicitly NOT fade-up (flagged default — avoided).
- **Hover personality (ONE): `ink-sweep`** — one background-size ink language in two
  weights: text links get **underline-draw** (0%→100% left-to-right), buttons get
  **fill-sweep** (cobalt → `--cobalt-deep` pseudo-element scaleX). Arrow links add the
  150ms icon-nudge. Plate hover: the caption's title underline draws + the frame border
  color sweeps to cobalt — same ink language, no lift/tilt/zoom mixing.
  - *One documented flourish:* on `(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)` only, the portfolio
    screenshot slowly pans inside its frame (`object-position: top → bottom`, 6s ease,
    returns on leave). Justification: it previews the actual site content below the fold —
    functional demonstration, not decoration. Cut it before cutting anything else if
    performance suffers.
- **Scroll set-piece (AT MOST ONE): `gallery hang`** — plates use CSS scroll-scrub
  (`@supports (animation-timeline: view())`): enter at `rotate(-1.2deg) scale(.97)
  opacity(.5)` → settle straight and full — frames being hung level on the wall.
  No-support fallback: static (perfect degradation). No JS scroll-jacking.
- **Continuous motion (≤2):** the hero marquee strip (translateX loop, 32s, pause on
  hover) + the coming-soon asterisk rotation (40s). Both stop under reduced motion.
- **Count-up: NOT USED** (flagged default; also we have no stats — honesty and taste
  agree here).
- **Gates (all mandatory):** everything inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`
  kill switch (reveals render visible, loops freeze); IO reveals fire once (unobserve,
  threshold .12); stagger caps ≤12 items; hover moves behind
  `(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`; transform/opacity only.
