Our Philosophy

We don't just cook steak.
We honor it.

01

Prime Selection

Every cut is hand-sourced from boutique ranches across the Pacific Northwest and Midwest, chosen for marbling, texture, and provenance.

02

Dry-Age Mastery

Our in-house dry-aging program runs 28 to 60 days in a custom Himalayan salt-lined chamber — developing depth no commercial cut can match.

03

The Fire

We cook exclusively over a custom wood-fired grill using Pacific alder and applewood — delivering a crust and char that gas simply cannot replicate.

Our Story

Born from obsession.

The Founder

Corey Blake

I grew up watching my father tend a backyard grill with the same reverence most people reserve for fine art. That obsession never left me.

After training at the Culinary Institute of America and spending a decade in New York's Michelin-starred kitchens, I returned to the Pacific Northwest with one mission: to open a steakhouse that treated beef as the nuanced, complex ingredient it truly is.

We opened in Belltown in 2010 with 42 seats and a single wood-fired grill. Fourteen years later, the grill is still the center of everything we do.

The Journey

14 years of pursuit

2010

The Beginning

Corey Blake's opens in Belltown with 42 seats and a singular focus on wood-fired perfection.

2012

James Beard Nomination

Named a semifinalist for Best New Restaurant, bringing national attention to Seattle's dining scene.

2015

Dry-Age Program Launched

Installation of our Himalayan salt-lined aging chamber — the first of its kind in Seattle.

2018

Private Dining Addition

Expansion of the space adds a 30-seat private room and a dedicated sommelier program.

2022

A5 Wagyu Program

Partnership with Miyazaki Prefecture producers brings Japan's finest beef to Seattle's table.

2024

Best Steakhouse in Seattle

Named Seattle Magazine's #1 steakhouse for the fourth consecutive year.
