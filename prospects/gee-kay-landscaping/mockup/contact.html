The company

Forty-five years, one town

Gee-Kay Landscaping, Inc. started in 1981. It's been a family-run landscape construction and maintenance company the whole time, owned by George Reinhardt, and the Reinhardts are Livingston natives.

The work hasn't changed shape since: lawn maintenance, design and installation of new landscapes and hardscapes, and upgrading the ones already in the ground.

Reviews on Angi put the company at 5.0. It holds a New Jersey Home Improvement Contractor license.
