Why Gee-Kay

Livingston natives, meticulous by habit.

The Reinhardt family started Gee-Kay in 1981 and never left town. Forty-five years later, the work is still done the same way neighbors describe it — meticulous, family-run, and fairly priced.

It is a family business that, in one customer's words, “performs like a well-oiled machine.” That is the whole idea: know the town, know the yards, and keep them the way you would keep your own.

Our story

The Reinhardt family founded Gee-Kay Landscaping in 1981 — Livingston natives building a landscape construction and maintenance company in their own town.

Forty-five years on, it is still run the same way: a family operation that handles both the building and the upkeep — new landscapes and hardscapes installed, existing ones maintained and upgraded, year after year. What the neighbors keep saying about the work has stayed remarkably steady, too: meticulous, family-owned, fairly priced.
