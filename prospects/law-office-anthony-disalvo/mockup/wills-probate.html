Wills, trusts and probate in Bloomfield, NJ

Most people put this off. Nobody wants to sit and think about dying, and nobody wants to do it across a desk from someone who makes them feel foolish for asking. He drafts the document in language that holds up, and he explains what each part of it does.

Wills and trusts

Estate planning for clients of modest means and clients of large ones. If you want it, he can also serve as executor of the will or administrator of the trust.

  • A last will and testament, drafted after a full conversation about what you actually want
  • Powers of attorney and advance directives for health care, including a do-not-resuscitate directive if you choose one
  • Witnessing done properly, so the will stands up
  • Affidavits that make the will harder to challenge later
  • Trusts — sprinkle, special needs, revocable, spendthrift, and whatever else the situation needs
  • Guardians for minor children, named properly
  • Home and hospital visits for clients who are near the end and need their plans finished
  • Copies of your documents kept at the office
  • Litigation if someone contests the will or the trust

Probate and estate administration

He takes on probate even when he didn’t draft the will. He works alongside executors and administrators, or serves in the role himself.

Start sooner rather than later. Courts set deadlines, and assets can disappear while nobody is in charge of the estate. You can hand him as much or as little of the executor’s job as you want to.

  • Submitting the will to the county Surrogate’s Court, the office that formally accepts it
  • Requesting letters of administration
  • Making sure every document that has to be found is found
  • Paying the bills and taxes, including final medical bills, funeral bills, and New Jersey Inheritance Tax
  • Making claims on life insurance policies
  • Distributing assets to the beneficiaries the will names
  • Dissolving the estate
  • Litigating any disputes that come up
