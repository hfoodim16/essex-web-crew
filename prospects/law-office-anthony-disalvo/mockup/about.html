Anthony DiSalvo, Esq.

One attorney, one office, at 1083 Broad Street in Bloomfield. Admitted in New Jersey in 2000.

He was a prosecutor before he opened this office, and before that a litigator at a national business law firm. He has worked in almost every kind of firm there is, from a large business litigation practice down to a two-person office.

Most of what he does now is smaller and closer to home: a closing, a will, a variance for someone’s kitchen extension. He speaks fluent Italian and welcomes Italian- and Spanish-speaking clients, and if you can’t get to Broad Street he’ll arrange to come to you.

He keeps costs down where he can, and he would rather settle a matter than try it when settling gets you to the same place. When it does need to be tried, he tries it.
