Real estate closings in Bloomfield and Essex County

Buying, selling, or leasing. He handles the contract review, the title work, and the closing table. You can reach him by phone or text while it’s happening.

For buyers

Residential closings for buyers, first-time buyers included. Contract review, title, and the closing itself.

For sellers

Residential closings for sellers. That includes estate sales run from out of state: he has handled one for a client selling a parent’s house from another state.

Commercial leasing

Commercial leases for owners and for tenants. Drafting, review, and the disputes that come out of them.

Landlord and tenant matters
