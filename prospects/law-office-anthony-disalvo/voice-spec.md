# Voice spec — Law Office of Anthony DiSalvo, Esq.

> **Dossier-only build.** No `client-answers.md` exists and none is coming (Harry
> authorized building from the dossier). Voice mined from: (a) the verbatim client
> reviews the Analyst captured, (b) his own recovered tagline and Yelp "About", and
> (c) his 2010 site prose — which was FindLaw-drafted *with* him, so it's a fact source
> and a weak voice signal, not a register to imitate. The reviews are the strongest
> evidence of how he actually operates: plain-spoken, refuses jargon, never pushy,
> honest about money.

**Register:** Professional office (Tier 2) — plain but composed, contracted, concrete.
Bent further toward plain than most attorneys: his clients' single loudest theme is
*"Lawyers like to fast talk and use big words — not here."* The copy must BE that, not
claim it.
**Say-aloud persona:** Would Anthony say this to a homeowner sitting across his desk on
Broad Street? He talks people OUT of paying him — nothing on this site oversells.

## How he writes / how this site sounds
Plain full sentences, contractions on, no flourish. Second person default ("your
closing", "your will"). Sentences a homeowner repeats to their spouse afterward.

## Watch list (on top of banlist Tier 2)
- **"experienced" / "experience"** — the 2010 FindLaw copy's crutch (appears in nearly
  every paragraph). Cap: twice per page. Prefer the concrete fact: "practicing since
  2000", "a former prosecutor".
- **"approachable", "caring", "personable"** — the 2010 site *claims* these; the reviews
  *prove* them. Never self-claim a personality trait. Show it: plain sentences, the real
  review, "weekend appointments", "reachable by phone or text."
- **"aggressive", "fight for you", "advocate", "trusted", "dedicated"** — wrong man,
  wrong register. His brand is calm.
- **No legal jargon anywhere in body copy** — no "pursuant to", "in the matter of",
  "utilize", "retain counsel". Necessary terms of art (variance, probate, executor,
  Surrogate's Court, letters of administration) get used once and explained in plain
  words the same sentence.
- **Never state a founding year.** Three conflicting published years (2000/2009/2010).
  The only safe era fact is the hard-sourced bar admission: "practicing law in New
  Jersey since 2000."
- **Never promise an outcome.** Traffic/zoning copy states the goal ("the goal is to
  reduce or dismiss the charge"), never a result.
- **No email address, no invented hours, no bar number** — placeholders or silence.

the differentiation is carried typographically (see plan §hero). **Do not** append a
year fragment or restructure it into three fragments — that's the sibling skeleton.
