# Outreach Log

Harry's record of every contact with a prospect. **This file is Harry's, not the team's** —
agents read it for context, agents never write to it, and the crew never contacts anyone.

One row per prospect, updated as it moves. Status values: `not contacted` → `contacted` →
`questionnaire sent` → `answers in` → `building` → `delivered` → `live`, or `closed` with
the reason.

## Notes

- A prospect that's gone silent 5–7 days after the first touch gets **one** follow-up
  (max two touches total), then it's closed. See PLAYBOOK.md Steps 9A–9C.
- Keep the reason on a close — a "no, we already have a guy" is worth different follow-up
  a year out than a "no, we're retiring."
