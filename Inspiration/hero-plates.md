# Inspiration/ — hero plate catalog

What lives in this folder, and which files can seed a hero-video shot.

**Read before using any of it:**

- These are **collected reference, not licensed stock.** No file here ships as a still in a
  client build. They seed image-to-video, and the output must be a **transformation** — a
  clip a viewer would recognize as the same photograph is a fail. Rule and rationale:
  `~/.claude/skills/web-design-ultra/references/inspiration.md`.
- **Every generation needs Harry's yes**, per `docs/media-policy.md`. Nothing here is
  pre-approved. The planner may mark one `VIDEO` slot as a request; that is all.
- **The site budget is all-in: $1.00 with no video, $1.50 with one — images included.**
  Price the run before asking. If it does not fit, spec a smaller shape or use the free
  rungs.
- **Try the free rung first.** A generic outdoor plate (wind, water, clouds, foliage) is
  usually available as a free-licence stock clip from Pexels or Coverr — boomerang and
  compress it locally per the hero-video-cover recipe in `video.md` and spend $0. Reach for
  generation only when the shot has to be specific.
- Prompt discipline, markup, CSS, the boomerang loop build and the ken-burns fallback all
  live in `~/.claude/skills/web-design-ultra/references/video.md`.

## The 6 photo plates

### 1. `45dd02a7fd6e18ebd91832b5efa04ffb.jpg` — backhoe pouring dirt
1080×1920 (9:16). Low-angle CAT backhoe mid-dump, soil streaming from the bucket, hard sun
flare behind the cab, blown-out sky.
**Register:** `filmed-action` · **Trade:** excavation, grading, site work, demolition
**Loop:** falling soil is continuously self-similar — boomerangs cleanly.
**⚠ Brand:** the CAT wordmark is legible on the boom. Real-brand likeness is banned —
reframe past it or specify an unbranded machine in the prompt. Do not rely on negation.
> Locked-off low camera looking up at a yellow excavator arm mid-dump. Dry soil pours in a
> continuous stream from the bucket, fine dust drifting across a hard backlit sun flare.
> Late-afternoon sun, warm dust haze, heavy machine stillness. Continuous loopable motion —
> the soil never starts or stops falling, nothing enters or exits frame. No text, no
> letters, no logos, no brand markings on the machine, no signage, no decals, no visible
> people, no vehicle plates.

### 2. `f17e8c549f9f0c3905c9061be73a6240.jpg` — excavator at trench edge
1080×1920 (9:16). Excavator perched on a cut bank above an open trench, arm and bucket
lowered into it, big cumulus sky, red-and-tan exposed earth strata.
**Register:** `filmed-action` · **Trade:** excavation, utilities, foundations, septic
**Loop:** cloud drift only — very stable, ideal boomerang candidate.
**⚠ Brand + people:** an operator is faintly visible in the cab. Compose the cab out or
specify an empty machine.
> Static wide shot of a yellow excavator parked at the edge of an open trench, bucket
> resting in the cut. Cumulus clouds drift slowly across a bright blue sky; a faint heat
> shimmer rises off the exposed red-and-tan earth. The machine is still. Continuous
> loopable motion, nothing enters or exits frame. No text, no letters, no logos, no brand
> markings, no signage, no people, no plates.

### 3. `5814a4d3c3c17b82629143fc6f6fa9ad.jpg` — grass seed-heads, close
736×1308 (9:16). Backlit ornamental grass seed-heads arcing against a soft blue sky, deep
green lawn falling away behind, shallow depth of field.
**Register:** `filmed-action` · **Trade:** landscaping, lawn care, garden design
**Loop:** the single best plate here. Wind sway is inherently oscillatory — boomerangs
invisibly. **Try the free stock rung first; this shot is generic enough that Pexels almost
certainly has it.**
> Locked-off macro shot of backlit ornamental grass seed-heads in the foreground, deep
> green lawn softly out of focus behind. A light breeze moves the seed-heads in a slow
> continuous sway, catching rim light on each stem. Bright midday sun, soft blue sky.
> Continuous loopable motion — the breeze never starts or stops, nothing enters or exits
> frame. No text, no letters, no signage, no buildings, no people.

### 4. `fd7a9c2fa78e1a63ef8c1fc836f2d2aa.jpg` — wildflower meadow, golden hour
1000×1500 (2:3). Dense perennial planting — coneflowers, alliums, ornamental grasses — with
sun rays breaking through a tree line behind.
**Register:** `filmed-action` · **Trade:** garden design, landscape architecture, nursery,
premium residential landscaping
**Loop:** flower sway plus light shimmer, both oscillatory. Strong candidate.
**Note:** the richest plate for a premium/design-led register, where plate 3 reads more
maintenance-and-mowing.
> Locked-off shot of a dense perennial wildflower meadow at golden hour — coneflowers,
> alliums and ornamental grasses. A light breeze moves the blooms in a slow continuous
> sway; low sun rays break through the tree line behind, shimmering through the stems.
> Warm backlight, soft haze. Continuous loopable motion, nothing enters or exits frame. No
> text, no letters, no signage, no buildings, no people, no garden tools.

### 5. `661221852b16cedeafcbabf1e26f62fd.jpg` — mountain lake and dock
704×1408 (9:16). Still alpine lake, wooden dock at the left edge, forested slopes and a lit
peak mirrored perfectly in the water.
**Register:** `filmed-action` · **Trade:** premium/outdoor, hospitality, wellness, real
estate — **not** a trade plate
**Loop:** water ripple and reflection drift, endlessly self-similar. Very stable.
**Note:** high risk of reading as generic stock wallpaper. It sells mood, not work — it
fails the frame-2 test for any business whose work is physically visible. Use it only where
atmosphere IS the pitch.
> Locked-off wide shot of a still alpine lake with a wooden dock at the left edge. Fine
> ripples move continuously across the surface, gently breaking the mirrored reflection of
> the forested slopes and sunlit peak. Early morning light, cool air, soft haze on the
> water. Continuous loopable motion, nothing enters or exits frame. No text, no letters, no
> signage, no boats, no people.

### 6. `ae63e006ee9e7b3dd32e6c0e066fe948.jpg` — Earth through a spacecraft porthole
1080×2400 (9:20). Fisheye view of Earth's limb through a station window, black space above,
metal window frame ringing the shot.
**Register:** `designed-loop` territory · **Trade:** tech, SaaS, studio, premium/creative
**Loop:** cloud drift and slow orbital roll — stable.
**⚠ Occupational fit gate applies.** A cosmic hero behind a local trade, legal or medical
prospect is exactly the convention error `color-conventions.md` exists to prevent. Hard no
outside the tech/studio register.
> Locked-off view of Earth's cloud-covered limb seen through a circular spacecraft window,
> deep black space above the horizon. Cloud systems drift slowly across the blue ocean; the
> horizon rolls almost imperceptibly. Cold sunlight, high contrast, the metal window frame
> static in the foreground. Continuous loopable motion, nothing enters or exits frame. No
> text, no letters, no logos, no signage, no visible people.
