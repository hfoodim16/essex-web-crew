# Inspiration — grounding design in real evidence

Purpose: stop inventing designs in a vacuum. Study real, high-quality sites in the niche, extract the *patterns* that make them work, and carry those into your directions. **Extract patterns, never clone a specific site.**

## Where to look (by site type)

| Site type | Primary galleries | Also |
|-----------|-------------------|------|
| Marketing / landing / brand | Awwwards (awwwards.com), Godly (godly.website), Land-book (land-book.com) | Curated: lapa.ninja, onepagelove.com |
| SaaS / product | Land-book, SaaS Landing Page (saaslandingpage.com) | Real competitor product sites |
| Web app / dashboard | Mobbin (mobbin.com), Dribbble (dashboard tag) | Linear, Vercel, Stripe dashboards |
| Local business / trade | Real top competitors in that trade + city (search) | Awwwards for the bold reference |
| E-commerce | Awwwards commerce, Land-book | Real brand stores in the category |

Always include **1–2 real competitors** in the exact niche alongside the gallery picks — the galleries give you ambition, the competitors give you category expectations to deliberately beat.

## How to research

1. Open the browser pane (`preview_start` with a `url`, or navigate the browser tools).
2. Visit 3–5 references. For each: screenshot the hero and one interior section, and read the page.
3. Do **not** just admire — dissect. Fill the evidence sheet below for each.
4. Note what's *overused* in the niche (the cliché to avoid) as explicitly as what's good.

## Evidence sheet (fill per reference)

```
Site: <name / url>
Layout archetype: <e.g. split-screen, editorial asymmetric, bento, immersive scroll>
Hero treatment: <headline scale, media type, layout, what grabs you>
Type system: <serif/sans/mono mix, size contrast, weight, casing>
Color story: <dominant, accent, how contrast is used, light/dark>
Background/depth: <flat? gradient mesh? texture? imagery? motion?>
Motion character: <subtle/kinetic; what animates and how>
One thing to steal (as a pattern): <the transferable idea>
One thing to avoid (niche cliché): <what everyone does that's boring>
```

## Synthesis (before Stage 5)

After the sheets, write 3–5 bullets: the patterns worth carrying forward, and the niche clichés you will deliberately break. This synthesis feeds directly into the divergence axes in `directions.md`.

## Rules

- Minimum 3 references, at least one from a top-tier gallery (ambition) and one real competitor (context).
- Extract transferable patterns, never a 1:1 copy of layout + color + type from one site.
- If a niche is visually stale (most local-trade sites are), that's the opportunity — name the cliché and beat it.
