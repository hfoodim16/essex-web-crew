# Inspiration — grounding design in real evidence

Purpose: stop inventing designs in a vacuum. Study real, high-quality sites in the niche, extract the *patterns* that make them work, and carry those into your directions. **Extract patterns, never clone a specific site.**

## Where to look (by site type)

| Site type | Primary galleries | Also |
|-----------|-------------------|------|
| Marketing / landing / brand | Awwwards (awwwards.com), Godly (godly.website), Land-book (land-book.com) | Curated: lapa.ninja, onepagelove.com |
| SaaS / product | Land-book, SaaS Landing Page (saaslandingpage.com) | Real competitor product sites |
| Web app / dashboard | Mobbin (mobbin.com), Dribbble (dashboard tag) | Linear, Vercel, Stripe dashboards |
| Local business / trade | Real top competitors in that trade + city (search) | Awwwards for the bold reference |
| E-commerce | Awwwards commerce, Land-book | Real brand stores in the category |

Always include **1–2 real competitors** in the exact niche alongside the gallery picks — the galleries give you ambition, the competitors give you category expectations to deliberately beat.

## How to research

1. Open the browser pane (`preview_start` with a `url`, or navigate the browser tools).
2. Visit 3–5 references. For each: screenshot the hero and one interior section, and read the page.
3. Do **not** just admire — dissect. Fill the evidence sheet below for each.
4. Note what's *overused* in the niche (the cliché to avoid) as explicitly as what's good.

## Evidence sheet (fill per reference)

```
Site: <name / url>
Layout archetype: <e.g. split-screen, editorial asymmetric, bento, immersive scroll>
Hero treatment: <headline scale, media type, layout, what grabs you>
Type system: <serif/sans/mono mix, size contrast, weight, casing>
Color story: <dominant, accent, how contrast is used, light/dark>
Background/depth: <flat? gradient mesh? texture? imagery? motion?>
Motion character: <subtle/kinetic; what animates and how>
One thing to steal (as a pattern): <the transferable idea>
One thing to avoid (niche cliché): <what everyone does that's boring>
```

## Name what you see (the extraction lens)

Filling the sheet in freehand prose (*"the hero has a big picture and some tiles under it"*) loses the pattern. **Name it.** A named pattern is portable into a direction brief, checkable against the anti-repetition log, and buildable without re-deriving it. Vague description is how "study real sites" quietly degrades into "glance at real sites."

Use these names in the `Layout archetype`, `Hero treatment`, `Motion character`, and `One thing to steal` fields. If a reference does something genuinely not on this list, name it anyway and say why it's new.

**Hero paradigms** — split-screen · full-bleed image/video · centered statement · asymmetric offset · kinetic type hero · product-shot hero · scroll-triggered reveal hero · interactive/reactive canvas hero · editorial masthead

**Navigation** — sticky condensing bar · transparent-over-hero · full-screen overlay menu · sidebar/rail · floating pill · mega-menu · dock magnification · scroll-progress nav

**Layout & grid** — bento grid (asymmetric tile grouping) · editorial asymmetric · Swiss/modular grid · masonry · card grid · alternating image+text (⚠ the zigzag — cap it, see `critique.md`) · full-bleed sectioning · sidebar-anchored · service index / ledger

**Cards & containers** — elevated card · flat bordered · glassmorphism panel · gradient-border · hover-expand · stacked/layered · tilt-parallax card · bare (no container — grouped by space alone)

**Scroll behaviour** — sticky scroll stack · horizontal pan · pinned statement · parallax layers · scroll-scrub timeline · progress indicator · reveal-on-enter · theme transition on scroll

**Galleries & media** — coverflow carousel · marquee strip (⚠ max one per page) · lightbox grid · before/after slider · scroll-snap rail · full-bleed plate sequence

**Typography moves** — oversized display · split-line mask reveal · character cascade · variable-weight shift · text scramble · outlined/stroke type · mixed-register (display + mono) · vertical/rotated labels

**Micro-interactions** — magnetic button · underline draw · fill sweep · icon nudge · cursor-follow label · lift-and-tilt · glyph scramble · pressed state

**Background & surface** — mesh/aurora gradient · grain/noise overlay · dot-grid or graph paper · topographic contours · spotlight follow · constellation/particle field · imagery-driven with scrim · flat token surface

> The vocabulary is a shared naming system, not a menu to shop from. Naming a pattern in an evidence sheet records what a real site actually did; it does not license dropping that pattern into the build. What you carry forward is decided in Stage 5 against the divergence axes and the ban list.

## Synthesis (before Stage 5)

After the sheets, write 3–5 bullets: the patterns worth carrying forward, and the niche clichés you will deliberately break. This synthesis feeds directly into the divergence axes in `directions.md`.

## Rules

- Minimum 3 references, at least one from a top-tier gallery (ambition) and one real competitor (context).
- Extract transferable patterns, never a 1:1 copy of layout + color + type from one site.
- If a niche is visually stale (most local-trade sites are), that's the opportunity — name the cliché and beat it.
- **Every sheet field gets a named pattern**, not a description. An evidence sheet full of prose is a sheet you can't diverge from in Stage 5 or check against `design-memory.md` in Stage 4.
