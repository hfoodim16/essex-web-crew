# Website Questionnaire

Hi [Name],

I'm Harry — I build websites for local businesses here in Essex County (Cecere Brothers
Landscaping is one of mine). Before I design anything for [Your business], I'd rather
hear what **you** want than guess. That's what these questions are for.

**Answer whatever you can and skip anything that doesn't apply — short answers are
completely fine.** Even a few words per question gives me plenty to work with.

---

## About your business

1. What's the business name, exactly as it should appear on the site?
2. How long have you been operating, and what's the short story? (A sentence or two —
   who started it, how it grew.)
3. What makes you different from the other guys? (The thing you'd want every customer
   to know.)
4. Which towns or areas do you serve?

## What the site should do

5. What do you want the website to do for you? (Bring more calls, look more
   professional, stop answering the same questions over and over, show up on Google…)
6. Who are your best customers? (Homeowners, businesses, a certain town or type of
   job — whoever you'd like more of.)
7. How do most customers find you today? (Word of mouth, Google, Facebook, Nextdoor,
   truck signage…)

## Your services

8. List everything you offer — even the small stuff.
9. Which 3–5 services matter most, in order? (These go front and center.)
10. Is there anything you're phasing out or DON'T want advertised?
11. Any specials, discounts, or seasonal offers worth showing? (First-time discount,
    senior discount, referral program…)

## Your current site (skip this section if you don't have one)

12. What do you like about your current site?
13. What bugs you about it?
14. Is there anything on it that must be kept word-for-word?

## Look & feel

15. Pick around 3 words for how the site should come across. (Examples: professional,
    friendly, high-end, family-run, modern, established, no-nonsense…)
16. Any websites you like the look of? (Competitors or totally unrelated — anything
    that made you think "that looks good.")
17. Any colors you want — or definitely don't want?
18. Your logo and colors: keep what you've got, or is this a fresh start?

## Photos & proof

19. What photos of your work do you have? (Phone pictures are fine — before/afters,
    finished jobs, your crew, your truck.)
20. Anything you'd want photographed that you don't have pictures of yet?
21. Any reviews or testimonials you're proud of? (Google, Yelp, Nextdoor, a thank-you
    text from a customer — anything real.)
22. Licenses, certifications, insurance, memberships, awards — what should we show off?

## How customers reach you

23. When someone's ready to hire you, what should they do — call, text, fill out a
    form, book online? (What do you actually prefer?)
24. Which phone number and email should appear on the site? And what are your hours —
    plus an emergency/after-hours line if you have one.
25. Should the site show your physical address, or just the areas you serve?

## The details

26. What questions do customers always ask you? (These make a great FAQ.)
27. Any policies or fine print the site should state? (Payment types, deposits, service
    minimums, cancellation policy…)
28. Is there anything you do NOT want on the website?
29. Do you already own a website address (domain name)? If so, what is it?
30. Anything else on your mind — and what's the best way and time to reach you while we
    work on this together?

---

**What happens next:** I'll build a first version of your site from your answers, show
it to you, and we'll fine-tune it together until it's right. Nothing goes live until
you've seen it and you're happy with it.

Thanks — looking forward to it.
Harry
